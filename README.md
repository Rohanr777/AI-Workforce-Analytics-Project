## Overview
The AI Workforce Analytics Project is a Tableau-based analytics project that analyzes the impact of AI adoption and automation on workforce trends, layoffs, employee satisfaction, productivity, and regional workforce risks.

This project helps organizations understand workforce transformation using interactive dashboards and data-driven insights.

---

# Project Objectives

- Analyze workforce and layoff trends
- Evaluate AI adoption and automation impact
- Understand employee satisfaction and attrition
- Compare regional workforce risks
- Identify high-risk industries and departments
- Generate business insights for decision-making

---

# Dataset Information

- Companies: 10+
- Industries: 8
- Regions: 5
- Departments: 8
- Records Analyzed: 10 Lakh+

# Key Insights

- AI-focused industries show higher innovation and productivity.
- Workforce distribution remains balanced across Hybrid, Remote, and Onsite models.
- Gaming industry shows the highest layoff risk.
- Employee satisfaction remained relatively stable over time.
- Higher AI adoption aligns with increased productivity trends.
